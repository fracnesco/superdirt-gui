play it from Tidal. These pages are the long one - what each part does, why it
does it that way, and what SuperDirt does underneath.

The help files - select a class name and press cmd/ctrl + d - are the reference
for every method; these pages are the explanation around them. The
[Tidal examples](../examples/tidal/README.md) are where to go to hear any of it, with
`examples/tidal/boot.hs` as the boot file: in `lessons/`, files
0 to 23 and 38 one thing at a time, files 26 to 34 whole pieces of nine parts each,
one style each, meant to play at once. [examples/grooves/](../examples/grooves/) is
a study in rhythm, one idea pushed through variations to its limit, with the
functions that change a running pattern in place and crossfade scenes.
[examples/pdef/](../examples/pdef/) plays the same library from SuperCollider's own
patterns, with no Tidal: its [README](../examples/pdef/README.md) has the words side by side.
